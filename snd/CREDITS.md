# Sound

## scream.mp3

Source: Freesound #219817 by **saraonsins**, released under
**CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)**.
https://freesound.org/s/219817/  ·  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 waives all rights, so no attribution is legally required and this file may
be used commercially. It is credited here anyway, because a public repo should
be able to answer "where did this come from" without anyone having to ask, and
because a ticketed attraction that cannot show the provenance of its assets has
a problem later rather than no problem.

What was done to it, in `print70.py` (kept with the session scratch, not shipped):
a 1.60s cut starting on the gasp before the shriek, then a 1970 print chain -
band-limited to 150Hz-5.6kHz like an optical film soundtrack, a small narrow
lift at 3k, light tape saturation, mechanical wow and flutter, a built plate
reverb ringing 0.8s past the dry, and the noise floor of the medium. The source
is clean (0.8% of samples touch the ceiling, longest flat run four samples) so
nothing here is repair; the losses are the entire point.

Output: 2.40s, mono, 44.1kHz, 64kbps, 19.7KB, peak -1 dBFS.
