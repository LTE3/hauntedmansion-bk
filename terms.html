Terms

What you’re buying, who you’re buying it from, and what’s expected once you’re inside.

Who you’re buying from

Haunted Mansion BK is operated by Pulse Ticketing LLC. Pulse Ticketing LLC sells every ticket on this site, and payment is processed by Stripe.

What a ticket is

$20 for one ticket, $35 for two, $60 for four. Every ticket is sold for one specific night. Tickets are final sale. See Refunds & transfers for how transfers work and what happens if we cancel a performance.

Age

13 and over. Guests under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.

Contact & safety words

Contact is optional and guest-controlled. Choose a green wristband for brief hand or forearm contact, or red for none. No actor may touch your head, neck or face. Say SAFE to stop any contact, or EXIT to leave the experience at any time.

What to wear

Wear closed-toe shoes and clothing you can move in. Costumes are welcome, but full-face masks, props and weapons are not permitted.

Phones

Phones must stay away during the experience. Flash photography and recording are not allowed.

Content warning

Expect darkness, loud sound, strobe effects, fog, confined spaces, live actors and intense scares. This is not designed for young children.

Where it is

The house is in Bushwick, Brooklyn. The exact address is not published on this site — it is emailed to confirmed ticket holders after purchase.
