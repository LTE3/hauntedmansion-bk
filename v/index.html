Thirty-one front pages were built for the house. These are the seventeen still standing, shown the way nearly everyone will meet them: on a phone. 22 to 31 are the newest, each one built from something a phone already is: a lock screen, a text thread, a camera roll, a torch. 17 to 21 came before them and were the first built on a photograph of the actual house. 15 and 16 are the two before them that broke the format. The rest are in the repository’s history, not on the site.

These are live pages, not mockups. Every form on them writes a real row into the production waitlist table, so every submit button on every one of them is the real one.

The live page is still built from fourteen, the keyhole, which came down from this gallery with the rest of the first fourteen. It stays on the front door until one of these replaces it. The fifteen newest are the only ones built on the house itself.
