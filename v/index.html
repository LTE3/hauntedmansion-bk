Seven doors.
Fourteen came down.

Twenty-one front pages were built for the house. These are the seven still standing: the five newest, which are the first built on a photograph of the actual house rather than on a set dressed to look like one, and the two before them that broke the format. The fourteen that came down are in the repository’s history, not on the site. The numbers are the sequence they were built in, not a ranking, and every one of these still works.

These are live pages, not mockups. Every form on them writes a real row into the production waitlist table, so every submit button on every one of them is the real one.
